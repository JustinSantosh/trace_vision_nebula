Vehicle traceability from video

Find the vehicle in any traffic video.

TraceVision reads number plates across many frames, works out each vehicle’s type and colour, and checks them against the registry. Every answer comes with its evidence, and a character it can’t read stays ? instead of a guess.

What it does

Multi-frame plate readingEvery good frame votes on each character. Unclear ones stay ?. Registry matchingTop five candidates with a score and the reasons, or “no confident match”. Possible matchesA partly read plate gets a ranked shortlist of five. Type and colourTwo detectors for the type; colour read from the vehicle’s own pixels. Cross-camera linkingOne vehicle seen from two angles becomes one pooled identity. Wrong-side drivingFlagged on fixed and moving cameras, confirmed by a person. Records and challansVehicle record, e-challan history and a printable challan PDF. Evidence viewer and reportsPlay the clip with tracked boxes; HTML and PDF report per clip.
